# Tituli

A **titulus** gathers precepts by subject, citing each by its token or namespace, and rubric by the handle ※0 prescribes. `∋` marks one. Mint a durable titulus in the table below, an ad-hoc one in the session that needs it.

A titulus is a lens: a member may sit in several, each drawing on a different aspect of that member's jurisdiction.

A titulus is no provision: it confers no additional authority, in aggregate or for its members. Citing one applies its members, which rank among themselves as if each had been named; minting one mid-flight disturbs nothing.

`∋P1` marks a project-local titulus, the ambit letter keeping it clear of the universal `∋1`.
