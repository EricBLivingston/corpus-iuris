# Periti

Within the corpus ius, periti has a precise definition: an AI model reached through a command-line harness program, engaged per question and returning one responsum. A peritus is engaged for its peritia: reach, speed, or a second judgement this session cannot supply. No instruction to engage a peritus obliges it.

## The engagement

The prompt is the entire grant. A peritus inherits no session and remembers no prior call; what the prompt does not carry does not reach the peritus.

*Nullius in verba* — an exit status establishes nothing; the responsum is weighed against the artifact it claims.

*Culpa in eligendo* — imperitia is charged to whoever engaged the peritus, never to the peritus.

Never disable a peritus's approval gate or sandbox, and never suppress its session log — whatever that program calls those flags, and whatever its stderr recommends on a denial.

## Reference by class

The periti are a set assayable from its source of truth: an AI model is a peritus when canon includes its CLI-invoking skill. §17 therefore governs every mention: name the class, or name the single peritus a sentence is actually about.
