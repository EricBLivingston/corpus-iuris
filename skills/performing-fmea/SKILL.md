# Performing FMEA

FMEA over one candidate spend, deviating from the industrial form where it matters: a line of prose per limb, no ordinal scales, no RPN, no worksheet. It governs the decision to spend, not the shape of the output.

A dispatch this protocol itself directs — the statement sent to the Authorizing Official, and whatever the assessment protocol requires of the AO — is control-plane work, not a launch, and carries its own authorization.

Settle the scale first. **Micro** means no new subagent or delegate dispatch and no material beyond what is already in hand: a guard, a fallback, a re-read, one more file. It settles here and silently — nothing when the answer is don't, and when it is do, one clause in the response you deliver naming the limb that fired, never a note to yourself. Default is don't, and conceivable-only never buys a defense at any severity; in doubt, proceed undefended. Nothing at this scale earns a statement, and a protocol emitting a table per guard has reproduced the disease it was built to cure.

Any dispatch, or a sweep past what is in hand, is a **launch** — and work that crosses that boundary mid-flight becomes a launch at the crossing, before it goes further. Then, by role:
