# Performing FMEA

Failure mode and effects analysis over one candidate spend, deviating from the industrial form: a line of prose per limb, no ordinal scales, no RPN, no worksheet.

A dispatch this protocol itself directs — the statement sent to the Authorizing Official, and whatever the assessment protocol requires of the AO — is exempt (⊢4, ⊢5).

Settle the scale first. **Micro** means no new delegate dispatch and no material beyond what is already in hand: a guard, a fallback, a re-read, one more file. It settles here and silently — nothing when the answer is don't, and when it is do, one clause in the response you deliver naming the limb that fired, never a note to yourself. Default is don't; in doubt, proceed undefended. Nothing at this scale earns a statement.

A dispatch, or a sweep past what is in hand, that the charter does not grant is a **launch**; doctrine-prescribed work is exempt per ⊢4. A launch is ultra vires (※12); so is a remit expansion, which amends the remit and is no launch. A launch crossing its charter mid-flight becomes ultra vires at the crossing; inside a governed run an expansion of produced work is gated at the phase boundary instead (`fmea-request.md § Ratifying an expansion`).
