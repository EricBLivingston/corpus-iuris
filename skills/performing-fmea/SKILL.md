# Performing FMEA

Failure mode and effects analysis over one candidate spend, deviating from the industrial form where it matters: a line of prose per limb, no ordinal scales, no RPN, no worksheet. It governs the decision to spend, not the shape of the output. It fires the moment you notice yourself deciding to spend — four more files to rule something out, a forensic dispatch after a surprise, breadth against an input shape no caller produces — or to move a limit rather than stop inside it. It is how a charter is expanded; whether the work abided by the charter is `governing-work`’s question.

A dispatch this protocol itself directs — the statement sent to the Authorizing Official, and whatever the assessment protocol requires of the AO — is exempt (⊢4, ⊢5).

Settle the scale first. **Micro** means no new delegate dispatch and no material beyond what is already in hand: a guard, a fallback, a re-read, one more file. It settles here and silently — nothing when the answer is don't, and when it is do, one clause in the response you deliver naming the limb that fired, never a note to yourself. Default is don't, and conceivable-only never buys a defense at any severity; in doubt, proceed undefended. Nothing at this scale earns a statement, and a protocol emitting a table per guard has reproduced the disease it was built to cure.

A dispatch, or a sweep past what is in hand, that the charter does not grant is a **launch**; doctrine-prescribed work is exempt no wider than the provision claimed (⊢4). A launch is ultra vires (※12); so is amending a bound the asked-for work cannot land inside, which is ultra vires without being a launch. Work that crosses either boundary mid-flight becomes ultra vires at the crossing, before it goes further. Then, by role:
